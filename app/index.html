Roadmap to Vice Got a Price!

Around 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. One playthrough is enough — there are no difficulty settings, no New Game+, and no mission replay, but free roam carries on after the story.

Stage 1

Play the story

60–80 hours

Play the Prologue and Chapters 1–5 and take optional missions as they appear — many are only offered for a while. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app (200 needed for 100%), study animals with binoculars (100), and meditate with both characters. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see Missables) and steer clear of the Irredeemable criminal profile: it is permanent.

Earns: all 12 story trophies, plus most of the early collectibles and activities.

Stage 2

Finish the 100% checklist

~100 hours

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through what is left: every robbery, bank vault and gang operation, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's Target Decks, plus 70 of the 80 challenges. This is the longest stretch — and it pops A Long Road, the hardest trophy.

Earns: A Long Road, Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, You WAiNKer!, Maximum Withdrawal and most of the rest.

Stage 3

Strangers & Freaks and clean-up

~10 hours

Stranger missions are not part of 100%, but Strangers & Freaks needs 8 strands, and several trophies come from character quest lines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret). Finish those, then check the Tracker for anything left.

Earns: Strangers & Freaks, Road to Rehabilitation, This Was No Boat Accident, The Lone Rider, Supply & Demand, Spin On This!, Fair Game.

Save plan — read this before you start

  • There are 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave slot. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 in free roam.
  • There is no mission replay. A save is your only way back.
  • Keep a Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops — see Missables.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving for that session. Keep them off your trophy run.
  • Turn on progress and collectible notifications in Settings, and Criminal Profile notifications so you can watch the 500 actions for Actions Speak Louder.

What can actually go wrong

Only one trophy is possibly missable. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam.

Possibly missable · 1 of 55

Positive Mental Attitude Bronze · 10G

Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

The real count: 18 meditation spots across 10 locations — not 18 spots in one place. Meditating at a spot with Jason and Lucia together counts twice. Track it in Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations.

The Chapter 1 catch: the morning after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen. She walks out to the balcony at Jason's house to meditate — follow her and choose Accept. That meditation adds +1 to the counter but is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the other 18, and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it. PowerPyx earned the trophy after doing it, so do it anyway. It is gone after Chapter 1.

What saves you: keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. The last spot needs both characters together, which opens after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. And the 100% checklist only needs 15 of 18 meditations, so this cannot cost you A Long Road.

Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase — both flag this as the only possibly-missable trophy, and both say the Chapter 1 requirement is unconfirmed.

Everything else is safe

No other trophy can be missed. Story trophies unlock as you play, quest-givers who go quiet during the story (Kenton, for example) come back later, and every collectible and activity stays available in free roam after the credits. Optional missions with an hourglass icon do disappear — but there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs, so missing one changes nothing.

A Long Road — the 100% checklist

35 requirements. The checklist only appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story, but everything you do before then already counts. Nothing on it is missable, and it is closer to 60% of everything in the game — most things are only partly required.

Sources & honesty

Where this list comes from

Grand Theft Auto VI released on 19 November 2026. Rockstar has not published an official trophy list of its own, so the names, descriptions, grades and Gamerscore here are the ones PowerPyx (GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, 18 Nov 2026) and GTABase (GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Achievements List, 18 Nov 2026) both publish — their lists agree on all 55. Every per-trophy guide names its source in the Tracker.

What is still unconfirmed

Six trophies are labelled unconfirmed in the Tracker, and this guide says so instead of guessing: Fair Game (which animal pops it), In The Beginning… (exact steps), Capital Offense (exact steps — PowerPyx names the blue Banshee method), Bad Luck, I Guess (believed to be the Prologue Payday Loans store), The Truth Is Out There (believed to be a UFO questline), Ten Pin (believed to be a motorcycle melee kick). The Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude is likewise unconfirmed as a requirement — the guide tells you to do it anyway.

How this guide was written

Every guide is written in this guide's own words from the facts in those two sources. Nothing is copied from another guide. No Rockstar logo, artwork or font is used anywhere; the sunset, skyline and palms are original CSS/SVG art drawn for this guide.

About the numbers

55 trophies on PS5 (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze), 28 hidden, 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox Series X|S, where the platinum is a 0G Mythic achievement. Difficulty 4/10 and ~200 hours are PowerPyx's estimates. The 100% checklist counts are PowerPyx's (35 requirements).
