Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies. Three stages, in order.

Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours · Chapters 1–5

Play the five chapters in order and do the optional missions as they appear, since there is no mission replay and optional tasks feed the 100% checklist. Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you travel. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation the morning after Down to Earth, and keep a save at the start and end of each chapter: 15 manual slots, no mission replay. Keep cheats off — they disable trophies and saving. Avoid pushing your Criminal Profile to Irredeemable; it is permanent.

Stage 2 — The 100% checklist ~100 hours · After the credits

After the story, the checklist appears under Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. Work the 35 requirements in the Checklist tab: gang operations, bank jobs, vaults, shooting-range golds, drone races, target decks, and the partial counts for vehicles, animals, meditations, challenges, stunts, WAiNK scans, flamingos, card sets and stick-ups. Progress made before the credits already counts, and nothing on the list is missable. This stage is most of the platinum.

Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours · After the story

Finish the eight counting stranger strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed), mop up the small trophies — arcade games, bowling, the Rhino, the jet at VCIA — and check the unconfirmed ones against the latest reports from PowerPyx and GTABase. Then the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks on its own.

What can actually go wrong

Only one trophy is possibly missable. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam.

A Long Road — the 100% checklist

The checklist appears only after the story, under Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. Progress made before the credits already counts, and nothing on it is missable.
